# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Event Face Finder to be a respectful, practical, and privacy-conscious project.
Contributors and users are expected to communicate professionally and to treat others
with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete problems.
- Respect privacy and consent concerns around face recognition.
- Avoid sharing private photos, embeddings, caches, or match outputs in issues or pull
  requests.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or sensitive data without consent.
- Using project spaces to encourage unlawful surveillance or non-consensual tracking.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
block participants when behavior harms the project or its community.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately. Do not include
private photos, embeddings, or event data unless a maintainer explicitly requests a safe
minimal reproduction.
